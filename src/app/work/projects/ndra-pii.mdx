## Overview

NDRA-PII explores how AI-driven, semantic analysis can identify and flag personally identifiable information hidden inside unstructured documents — going beyond simple pattern matching to understand context and risk.

## Contribution

- Curated and preprocessed labelled datasets used to train and evaluate the PII detection system
- Supported benchmarking and validation of detection accuracy across varied unstructured document types
- Worked with NLP tooling including spaCy and Presidio for entity recognition and redaction workflows

## Technical Approach

The project applies natural language processing techniques to parse free-form text, identify PII entities, and assess distributed risk across a document set. Dataset curation and validation were central to ensuring the model generalizes across different formats and writing styles.

## Status

NDRA-PII is an ongoing project (Jan 2026 – Present), with continued work on dataset expansion and detection validation.
